OFC Pineapple Fantasyland EV Chart: Effective Stack & 54-Card vs 52-Card

Most published Open Face Chinese (OFC) Pineapple Fantasyland values assume an infinite stack and a 54-card deck (2 Jokers). On real 50pt–100pt app club tables or 52-card (No Joker) games, infinite-stack intuition severely overvalues Trips chases and undervalues locking QQ.

1. 54-Card (2 Jokers) Fantasyland EV by Effective Stack

Effective stack caps how many total points you can win from a single Fantasyland chain (including Re-Fantasy loops). Because 14-card QQ rarely chains long enough to hit a 100pt cap, its value barely moves—whereas 17-card Trips depends heavily on deep multi-hand chains and collapses on shallow stacks.

2. 54-Card (2 Jokers) vs 52-Card (No Jokers, ∞ Stack)

In 52-card Pineapple OFC (no Jokers), hitting a Top-row Trips or Bottom-row Quads to re-enter Fantasyland becomes dramatically harder. That drop in repeat probability compounds across chains and cuts every Fantasyland tier nearly in half.

4. Methodology & Reproducibility

All values above were computed using OFC Optimizer’s exact Fantasyland solver (1,200 randomly dealt hands per entry tier) coupled with a 100,000-chain simulation using same-card Re-Fantasy rules. Figures represent own-chain expected value only (matching standard OFC reference conventions; subtracting opponent counter-Fantasyland lowers absolute values further). Standard errors at ∞ stack: QQ ±0.7pt, KK ±1.2pt, AA ±2.5pt, Trips ±6.8pt.
