OFC Pineapple Rules & Royalties Chart

Everything you need at the table in one clean reference: how Open Face Chinese (OFC) Pineapple is dealt, complete Top / Middle / Bottom royalty tables, scoop & foul scoring, Progressive Fantasyland entry and repeat rules, plus free browser tools.

1. OFC Pineapple in 30 Seconds

  • Build 3 poker hands (13 cards): Top (3 cards), Middle (5 cards), and Bottom (5 cards).
  • Street 0 (Opening): Each player is dealt 5 cards and sets all 5 face up (no discard).
  • Streets 1–4: On each of the next 4 streets, draw 3 cards, place 2 face up, and discard 1 face down until all 13 slots are full.
  • No Fouling: Your final board must satisfy Bottom ≥ Middle ≥ Top. Breaking hand order results in a Foul, voiding your entire board and royalties.

3. Scoring, Scoops & Foul Penalties

Valid vs. Valid Board

1. Row points: +1 pt for each row won (Top, Middle, Bottom; 0 for ties).
2. Scoop bonus: Win all 3 rows for an extra +3 pts (total +6 pts before royalties).
3. Royalty net: Add your total royalties minus your opponent's total royalties.

Foul Penalty

If one player fouls, they score 0 royalties and pay the valid player 6 pts (scoop) + all of the valid player's royalties.
If both players foul, the hand is a 0-pt tie.

4. Progressive Fantasyland Entry, Repeat & True EV

  • Entry: Top QQ = 14 cards, KK = 15 cards, AA = 16 cards, Three of a Kind = 17 cards (set all 13 cards at once face down).
  • Repeat (Re-Fantasy): Complete Three of a Kind on Top OR Four of a Kind+ on Bottom without fouling.
  • 54-Card (2 Jokers) vs. 52-Card (No Jokers): Jokers dramatically increase repeat odds—in deep stacks, 17-card Trips FL drops from 126.4 pts (54-card) to 58.5 pts (52-card).

5. Joker Wildcard Rules (54-Card Mode)

In 54-card Joker OFC, each Joker acts as any card (even duplicating an existing rank/suit on the board). If the highest interpretation of a Joker would cause a foul against an adjacent row, the Joker automatically demotes to the strongest legal hand that preserves Bottom ≥ Middle ≥ Top.
