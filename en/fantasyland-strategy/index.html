2. The 4-Step Fantasyland Arrangement Checklist

Step 1: Scan for Repeat Routes First

Sort by rank immediately and check whether Bottom Quads+ or Top Trips (222+) is legally achievable without fouling. In 54-card 15–17 card hands, locking in the repeat almost always dominates.

Step 2: Abuse the 2× Middle Royalty Multiplier

Middle pays double Bottom (Flush +8 vs. +4, Full House +12 vs. +6). Whenever you can make two strong 5-card hands, push the highest legal one into Middle rather than burying it in Bottom.

Step 3: Climb the Top Pair Ladder (66 → AA)

Once Middle and Bottom are set, maximize your Top pair among the remaining cards—jumping from High Card (0) to AA (+9) is worth 9 pure points before even winning the row.

Step 4: Joker Priority: Repeat > Middle Monster > Top Pair

Use Jokers first to secure Re-Fantasy. If no repeat is reachable, deploy Jokers where they add the biggest single royalty leap (Middle Full House +12 / Quads +20 / Top AA +9).
