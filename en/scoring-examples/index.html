How to Score OFC Pineapple Poker: 4 Worked Examples

Every heads-up OFC Pineapple hand settles in three quick steps: (1) Row points, (2) Scoop bonus (+3), and (3) Net royalties. Here are the 4 real-table situations that trip players up—worked out step by step.

Example 1: Winning 2 of 3 Rows—and Still Losing by 19 Points

Player A wins both Middle and Bottom (2–1 on rows), yet Player B's monster Top Trips (KKK = +21 pts) flips the settlement into a 19-point win for B:

Row Player A Player B Winner
Top 66 (+1) KKK (+21) B
Mid A-high Straight (+4) 9-high Straight (+4) A
Bot A-high Flush (+4) Q-high Flush (+4) A
Step-by-step arithmetic:
1. Row points: A wins 2–1 = A +1 pt
2. Scoop: 0 pt
3. Royalty net: A (1+4+4 = 9) − B (21+4+4 = 29) = A -20 pts
👉 Final Settlement: +1 + (-20) = -19 pts (Player B wins +19 pts & enters 17-card Fantasyland!)

Example 2: Clean 3–0 Scoop (+6 pts) + Royalties

Player A: Top KK (+8) / Mid Trips (+2) / Bot Straight (+2) = 12 royalties
Player B: Top High Card (0) / Mid One Pair (0) / Bot Two Pair (0) = 0 royalties

• Row points: A wins all 3 rows = +3 pts
• Scoop bonus: +3 pts (total +6 pts for a scoop)
• Royalty net: 12 − 0 = +12 pts
👉 Final Settlement: A wins +18 pts (+ 15-card Fantasyland next hand)

Example 3: Single-Player Foul Penalty (-33 pt Swing)

Player A: FOULS (Top QQ, Mid Two Pair, Bot One Pair)
Player B: Valid (Top AA +9 / Mid Full House +12 / Bot Full House +6 = 27 royalties)

• Fouling voids all of Player A's royalties (Top QQ +7 becomes 0).
• Player B automatically receives +6 pts (scoop) + all 27 of B's royalties.
👉 Final Settlement: Player A pays Player B 33 pts (and B enters 16-card FL!)

Example 4: Double Foul (0-pt Tie)

If both players foul their boards, the hand is a 0-pt tie. Neither player collects royalties and neither enters or repeats Fantasyland.
